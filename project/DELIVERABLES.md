Rank hour-of-week windows by observed appliance kWh and compare with a lagged-demand forecast. **Interpretation boundary:** This is a residential proxy, not a commercial-building portfolio. Appliances and lights are Wh per interval; no measured occupancy, tariff, floor area or commercial comfort standard exists. Exclude random rv1/rv2. Same-interval sensor explanations are not advance forecasts.
